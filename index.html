Step 1 — Add official rate sources

Files are optional: the system automatically checks official bank pages and PDFs as well. Upload screenshots to fill gaps where those sites are dynamic or blocked. You can upload more than one document for a bank.

Use the latest eligible rate sheet that was effective on or before this date, even if it was published earlier. For example, an EBL sheet effective 1 November can be used on 18 November if still current.
Upload guidance: Include the product name, tenor columns, interest rates and the document's effective date. PDF text and screenshot OCR are processed temporarily; the system does not retain files or presentations. Unclear extracted rates are flagged instead of guessed.

Step 2 — Review and generate PowerPoint

The latest eligible official rates have been collected. Review unresolved banks and any OCR suggestions, then generate the editable presentation. MTB Proposed Rate will remain blank.
