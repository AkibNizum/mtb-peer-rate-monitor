Step 1 — Upload bank PDFs

The system does not search bank websites or reuse old rates. Upload one or more official rate-sheet PDFs per bank. Without a PDF, that bank's rate cells will show -. Need to use screenshots? Convert them to a PDF using the separate Screenshot → PDF tab.

Only the PDFs you provide will be considered. A document with an effective date after the selected report date will not be used.
Upload guidance: Provide bank-issued PDFs showing product names, tenors, amount brackets and effective dates. If a PDF is scanned, the page will try OCR. A rate not established from the supplied PDF is shown as -, without guessing. Proposed Rate stays blank. Uploaded files and presentations are not saved.

Step 2 — Review and generate PowerPoint

Only the supplied PDFs have been processed. Review extracted-rate suggestions if needed, then download the editable original-format presentations. Missing or unreadable rates show -; Proposed Rate remains blank.
