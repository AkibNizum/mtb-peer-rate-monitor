No account is needed. The report is generated fresh for each request.
Data accuracy: Uses the latest eligible official bank rate sheet dated on or before the selected date. Rates that cannot be verified from official publications are left blank and identified for review. MTB Proposed Rate always stays blank. Check the workbook’s “Source PDFs” and “Source Review” sheets for effective dates, PDF links and access issues.
Official rate sheet unavailable? Upload PDF or image (optional)

Upload a bank-issued PDF, JPG, or PNG if the official bank website blocks access. Files are processed temporarily for extracting evidence text, not saved. The report includes extracted rate-bearing text as unverified candidates until you check the official document.

For JPG/PNG, text recognition runs inside your browser. PDF text extraction runs temporarily on the server and is not archived.
